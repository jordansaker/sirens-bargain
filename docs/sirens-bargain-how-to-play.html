How to Play

⚑The Goal

Be the first to lay down three complete realm sets in front of you. Each realm needs a set number of cards — small ones like Tide Pools need 2, bigger ones like Ocean Currents need 4.

1Your Turn

Draw 2 cards to start (draw 5 if your hand is empty). Then play up to 3 cards: bank Pearls, lay down realms, or play action cards. End with 7 or fewer cards in hand.

♛Winning

The moment your third realm is complete, you win — even mid-turn. Opponents will try to steal your sets with actions, so a realm isn't safe until the game ends.

Realm cards

The properties you collect. Complete a set to make it count toward winning and to charge higher rent.

Wild & Pearls

Wild realms stand in for either of two realms to finish a set. Rainbow Conch counts as any realm. Pearls are money — bank them to pay rent and actions.

Tributes

Charge other players rent on a realm you own. Siren's Toll hits one chosen player on any realm.

Take & steal

Kraken's Grasp steals a whole finished set. Slippery Eel takes one loose card. Trade Winds swaps a card with an opponent.

Charge & defend

Toll of the Tides and Mermaid's Feast make others pay you. Siren's Refusal cancels an action played against you — and can itself be cancelled.

Build & boost

Coral Cottage and Pearl Palace raise a finished realm's rent. High Tide doubles your next Tribute. Ride the Current draws 2 extra cards.
